HOW TO READ THE ARCHIVE

Saved values, with their dates

Each archive entry preserves the race predictions saved for that release. Source dates and forecasting methods can differ between releases, so this page does not calculate forecast movement or attribute why two sets of predictions differ.

A day-by-day replay would need matching, timestamped inputs for every date. Those records are not available here. The History page compares forecasts with results across the 2018–2024 elections.

THE BIG PICTURE

Chamber outlooks

The homepage summarizes the listed 2026 House, Senate, and Governor races.

  • House: expected seats add the published Democratic win chances across 435 districts.
  • Senate: the outlook combines 35 listed races with 65 seats already held.
  • Governors: expected wins add the chances across the 36 on-ballot races.

Expected totals use probabilities, not hard race calls. Seat ranges are estimates and may not capture shared movement across races. A flip tag means the favored party differs from the previous result for that seat.

NATIONAL VOTE CONTEXT

House generic ballot

The homepage shows a cached polling average beside the national House vote estimate. Both are Democratic-minus-Republican margins. Poll fieldwork dates do not establish when results were first made public; see About the data for source timing and limits.

FIND A RACE

Explore races and states

On the homepage, choose an office, search by state or race, and sort by closeness or race name. The map colors summarize listed race chances within each state; they are not statewide vote forecasts. Select a state tile to filter the race list.

The State explorer adds state search, office filters, coverage counts, and quick links to the closest race. The state average describes listed races only.

ONE RACE AT A TIME

Race details

Open a race to see its margin, both party chances, rating, candidates, previous results, past forecasts, and available polls. Candidate names are kept separate from party-level chances.

Past election pages show available forecast values, outcomes, and poll averages. Some source archives have incomplete dates or race matches; missing values are left blank.

Election history

The History page includes interactive charts for margin MAE, winner accuracy, and probability scores. Filter by office to focus the charts and summary cards, then open the results table for race coverage, expected seats, and seat calls.

These historical results are descriptive comparisons across four elections, not guarantees of future performance. Races in the same election share conditions.

DATED FORECASTS

Forecast archive

The archive page has two views. Published forecasts compares saved releases as they appeared on their snapshot dates. Simulated history shows a reconstructed 2026-cycle forecast path from January 4, 2025 through the cutoff date shown on the page. It is not a record of forecasts published on those dates or an accuracy test against past outcomes. In the current replay, House values begin August 10, 2026; Senate and Governor have some missing dates. The page identifies these gaps and explains uncertainty in historical poll timing. A race page's View simulated history link opens the same chart with that race selected.

A COMPANION TO THE FORECAST

Artwork gallery

The gallery groups images by series. Select a thumbnail for a larger view; close it with the button, by clicking outside the image, or with Escape. Artwork is decorative and does not affect race chances.

For margin definitions, seat-range assumptions, and date limitations, see About the data.
